## Descriptions

- **Shelf Life (current config):** For you as served: item-kNN + ALS fold-in blend, fame-gated prediction boost, prediction floor; young adult included, no other content filters.
- **Best match as displayed (author variety):** The same model with the Recommendations page's display step: repeat authors nudged down (author_penalty=0.5). Diagnostic; not the model's score.
- **Previous best: Shelf Life (recency half-life 25):** Champion from 3f21323 (reports/2026-10-03_2141_shelf_life.md).
- **Random:** Uniformly random unread books from the For you universe (seeded per user).
- **Popular:** Most-read books among training users.
- **Genre + popularity:** Popular books within the parent genres the user rates most (rating-weighted genre share x within-genre popularity).
- **2023: similar readers:** The 150 most similar readers (cosine); their most-rated books with >100 ratings and average > 3.75. Re-implemented on this project's training data (work ids, this catalog); see goodrec/eval/legacy2023.py for the differences from the original.
- **2023: SVD:** SVD (k=42) of the 3,000 most similar readers' ratings, ranked by predicted rating; average > 3.5. What the 2023 web app served. Re-implemented on this project's training data (work ids, this catalog); see goodrec/eval/legacy2023.py for the differences from the original.
