## Track 2 · Rating prediction

How close is the predicted rating to the one the user gave each hidden book (all of them, 1–5★)? Every prediction uses only the user's visible ratings and training-only item statistics. MAE is the primary metric; lower is better.

### Metrics

MAE and RMSE in stars, averaged per user and then over users (RMSE: the root of the mean per-user squared error). MAE/σ: each user's MAE divided by the spread of their visible ratings (σ_u, shrunk toward the population's 1.05), so a one-star miss counts more for a reader who rates everything 4–5★. Pearson: correlation over all held-out ratings. Spearman: rank correlation within each user's hidden books, averaged over users with at least 3 hidden ratings that aren't all the same (92.3% of users; a method that predicts the same rating for every book scores 0). ±1★ / ±0.5★: share of predictions within one / half a star. Bias: mean prediction − actual. Spread: the SD of predictions as a share of the SD of actual ratings (below 100% = squeezed toward the middle). A method with no estimate for a book uses the book average; the share is in brackets.

## Descriptions

- **v4 · Taste model capped at 40% (re-scored):** For you as served: item-kNN + ALS fold-in blend, fame-gated prediction boost, prediction floor; young adult included, no other content filters. Rating: the predicted rating a book card shows.
- **Best match as displayed (author variety):** The same model with the Recommendations page's display step: repeat authors nudged down (author_penalty=0.5). Diagnostic; not the model's score.
- **Previous best: v3 · Fame-gated prediction boost (re-scored):** Champion from 8fd70c5 (reports/2026-10-04_0937_shelf_life.md).
- **Random:** Uniformly random unread books from the For you universe (seeded per user).
- **Popular:** Most-read books among training users.
- **Genre + popularity:** Popular books within the parent genres the user rates most (rating-weighted genre share x within-genre popularity).
- **2023: similar readers:** The 150 most similar readers (cosine); their most-rated books with >100 ratings and average > 3.75. Re-implemented on this project's training data (work ids, this catalog); see goodrec/eval/legacy2023.py for the differences from the original.
- **2023: SVD:** SVD (k=42) of the 3,000 most similar readers' ratings, ranked by predicted rating; average > 3.5. What the 2023 web app served. Re-implemented on this project's training data (work ids, this catalog); see goodrec/eval/legacy2023.py for the differences from the original.
- **Book average + user offset:** The book's average plus how far above or below book averages the user rates (b_u, shrunk by 5 pseudo-ratings). Knows harsh from generous raters.
- **Book average:** The book's average rating among training readers, shrunk toward its Goodreads average (the item mean every Shelf Life prediction starts from).
- **User's average rating:** The average of the user's visible ratings, for every book.
- **Goodreads average:** The book's Goodreads-wide average rating (2017 snapshot over all readers, including ratings after a user's split date; reference only).
- **Shelf Life, uncalibrated rating:** Ablation: the raw predicted rating (item mean + the user's offset + item-item residual), before calibration to the user's rating scale.
