What it does

Five things that take the busywork out of a job search.

📬

Daily matches

New postings from your chosen organizations and boards, every day, with the best in a short daily email.

🎯

Rated for you, with reasons

Each job gets an interest and a fit rating, with the phrases that decided them highlighted.

Interest: HighFit: Med
📝

Tailored resumes

Your own Word resume, edited for a job as tracked changes, so every change is yours to accept or reject.

💬

Jobby, your prep partner

A chat that knows the job and your background: mock interviews, likely questions, strategy.

📋

One place to track it all

Every application from draft to offer, with how your search is going and what's working.

How to start

It's a personal project shared with a small group, so accounts are approved one at a time.

  1. Sign in with Googleand send a short request. Nothing is shared with anyone yet.
  2. Get approvedby the owner. You'll get an email when you're in.
  3. Set up in a few minutesUpload your resume, say what you're looking for, and pick sources to follow.

Your information

  • The app keeps what you give it: your resumes and cover letters, profile and preferences, and the jobs you track. Only you can see them in the app.
  • To rate jobs, tailor documents and chat with Jobby, it sends the relevant parts to Anthropic's Claude API, which by default doesn't use API data to train its models.
  • The app's owner runs it and can reach the stored data, including backups. You can clear everything, or delete your account, at any time.
